# CompassCAD

Hey there! This is our new home for all things CompassCAD, now happily owned by [Boowat](https://github.com/boowat) (where the [original author of CompassCAD](https://github.com/zeankundev) now works)!

With this, as a separate organization for all things CompassCAD, we can deliver updates efficiently while focusing on our long-term mission of **making CAD easy for everyone**.

Here, you can find active projects, as well as scrapped variants of CompassCAD!

## 🤝 Contribution guidelines
If you want to contribute, you can! It's easy, just:
1. **Create a fork**, write your changes, and push your fork.
2. **Create a pull request** at one of our upstream projects
3. **State your changes in detail, and why**, and how does it affect the user experience (UX)
4. **Wait for one of our representatives** to come in and view your PR changes.
5. If all checks out, **we will merge your PR** and make your changes upstream!

> **Note:** Direct pushes to the upstream repository are restricted to Boowat employees, including `zeankundev`.

## 📦 Repositories
- `NextDesktop`: The CompassCAD NEXT desktop app
- `ReactWeb`: The CompassCAD NEXT web app, for both desktop and mobile. Accessible via [https://compass-cad.web.app](https://compass-cad.web.app)
- `Mobile`: ~Scrapped~ A special version of CompassCAD NEXT, built specifically for mobile platforms.
